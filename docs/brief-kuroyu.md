# Kuroyu 黒湯 — creative brief

**What:** a made-up 8-seat late-night tonkotsu ramen counter in Singapore. Concept site for a reel. Footer says it's a concept.

**The bar:** Awwwards Site of the Day. Henry rejected the last round as "horrible, nothing like the video". The video standard: a cinematic AI film that plays as you scroll, so the page feels like a film you drive with your thumb. If a section doesn't move or doesn't make you stop, it goes.

**The one moment:** "you scroll and the ramen builds itself in front of you."

## Feel
Quiet Japanese luxury after dark. Near-black (sample it from the clip, around #0b0907), bone-white type, ONE accent taken from the food (broth gold ~#d9a24b or the egg yolk orange; pick one). Editorial, slow, confident. Big type, lots of black, the food is lit like a jewel. Steam and grain tie it together.

Type direction: a high-contrast editorial serif for display (e.g. Cormorant Garamond / Instrument Serif / Fraunces at large sizes, tight tracking), a quiet sans or mono for small labels. 黒湯 in Shippori Mincho (Google Fonts) as a big vertical graphic element. No italic-word-in-sans gimmicks, no emoji, no em dashes.

## Scroll story (in order)
1. **The build (pinned, ~300vh).** `clip-build.mp4`: the empty black bowl fills itself (broth pours, noodles, chashu, egg, negi, nori, steam). Headline beside the bowl, never on it. Labels appear on the frame each part lands, one at a time, thin line to the part: Broth 18 hours · Noodles 0.9 mm, Hakata thin · Chashu torched to order · Ajitama 6 min 30 s · Negi · Nori. At the end the steam blooms and the wordmark lands.
2. **The broth.** `broth.png` macro, slow parallax / scale; a scroll-revealed line: "Pork bones at a rolling boil for eighteen hours, until the broth turns the colour of milk."
3. **The lift (pinned).** `clip-lift.mp4`: chopsticks lift the noodles in slow motion. One word, huge: "Lift." Short copy beside.
4. **The menu.** Three bowls (`menu-black.png` Kuro black garlic S$19, `menu-red.png` Aka spicy miso S$19, `menu-shio.png` Shio yuzu clear S$18, plus the hero bowl Kuroyu tonkotsu S$17). Horizontal or staggered editorial layout, not three equal boring cards.
5. **The counter.** `counter.png`: "Eight seats. Last bowl at 2am." Hours 6pm to 2am, Tue to Sun. Location: Keong Saik, Singapore (no street number).
6. **Close.** One button, one label: "Book a seat". Footer: "Kuroyu is a concept by Henry Chua. Not a real restaurant."

## Assets (all AI, Higgsfield: GPT Image 2.5 + Kling 3.0)
`clip-build.mp4` (10 s, last frame = `start.png` finished bowl, first frame = `end_a.png` empty bowl), `clip-lift.mp4` (10 s), `broth.png`, `counter.png`, `menu-*.png`, `start.png`.

## THE STORY (Henry, 7 Oct: the site must tell one story; animation and words follow it)

**"Eighteen hours, one bowl."** The page is one night at Kuroyu, told by the clock. A small clock in the corner (mono, e.g. `08:00`) is the narrator: it runs forward as you scroll, and every chapter starts at a time.

- **Cold open (build clip, pinned).** Black screen, the empty bowl. Line: "Every bowl here takes eighteen hours. You'll finish it in eight minutes." As you scroll, the clock runs 08:00 → 23:40 in step with the clip, and each label lands with its time:
  - 08:00 Bones in. (broth pours)
  - 14:00 The broth turns white.
  - 21:30 Noodles cut, 0.9 mm. (noodles land)
  - 23:38 Egg, 6 min 30 s. / Chashu, torched to order. (toppings land)
  - 23:40 Served. (steam blooms, wordmark 黒湯 Kuroyu lands)
- **23:41 The first lift (lift clip, pinned).** "Lift." Then: "Nobody talks for the first minute."
- **Why it's white (broth.png).** Back to 08:00 for one chapter: the story of the bones at a rolling boil until the fat and collagen turn the broth to milk.
- **The four bowls (menu).** Written as "what the regulars order", one line of character each.
- **02:00 Last bowl (counter.png).** "Eight seats. Last bowl at two." Hours, location.
- **Close.** "Your seat is still warm." Button: Book a seat.

The clock, the labels, the headlines and the motion all serve this one night. No section exists that isn't part of it.
