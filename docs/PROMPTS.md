# Every prompt used for the film

All made on Higgsfield. Stills: GPT Image 2.5 (quality high, 2K). Film: Kling 3.0 (mode pro, 10 s, sound off, 16:9). Total for both sites, including retries: about 120 credits.

The trick: make the **first and last frame as stills first**, then give Kling both. It only has to invent the motion in between, so the end of the scroll lands exactly on your hero shot.

---

## Kuroyu (ramen)

**1. Hero still (the finished bowl, last frame of the build)**
> Photoreal editorial food photograph, shot on Phase One medium format. A single bowl of rich tonkotsu ramen in a matte black stoneware bowl, centered in the frame, three-quarter overhead angle (about 40 degrees), on a dark charred-wood counter. Creamy opaque broth, neat fold of thin noodles, two slices of torched chashu pork with caramelised edges, a halved soy-marinated egg with a jammy orange yolk, one sheet of nori standing at the back, a tight pile of finely sliced green spring onion, a few drops of black garlic oil. Soft wisps of steam rising, lit from behind. Single warm side key light from the left, background falls off to pure near-black, no props, no chopsticks, no text, no logo. Bowl occupies the middle 45 percent of the frame with generous dark space on both sides. Moody, premium, cinematic.

**2. Empty bowl (first frame), hero still as reference**
> Exactly the same photograph as the reference: identical camera angle, framing, lens, bowl position and size, charred wood counter, warm side light from the left and near-black background. The only change: the matte black stoneware bowl is now completely empty and clean. No broth, no noodles, no toppings, no nori, no steam. Just the empty black bowl sitting in the same spot, its dark glazed inside catching a soft highlight. No text.

**3. The build (Kling, start = empty bowl, end = hero)**
> Locked-off static camera, no camera movement at all. The empty black ramen bowl fills itself, stop-motion style, no hands, no people. First a stream of hot creamy tonkotsu broth pours in from above and rises to the rim. Then a neat fold of thin noodles drops into the broth. Then two slices of torched chashu pork land on the right, a halved jammy egg lands on the left, a pile of sliced spring onion drops in the middle, and finally a sheet of nori slides in at the back. Drops of black garlic oil bead on the surface and soft steam begins to rise. Photoreal, warm side light, consistent lighting, smooth motion, no text.

**4. The lift (Kling, start = hero)**
> Locked-off static camera. A pair of dark wooden chopsticks enters from the top of frame, dips into the bowl and slowly lifts a long tangle of glossy thin noodles high out of the creamy broth in slow motion. Broth drips back down in glistening strands, thick steam billows up through the warm side light. Ultra slow motion 120fps feel, photoreal food commercial, shallow depth of field, near-black background, no hands visible beyond the chopsticks, no text.

**5. The broth (Kling 5 s, start = a boiling-pot still)**
> Locked-off macro shot, no camera movement. Milky white tonkotsu pork broth at a hard rolling boil in a black stockpot: big bubbles rise and burst across the creamy surface, the broth churns and folds, thick steam rolls up through warm backlight. Food commercial, photoreal, near-black background, no text.

Menu bowls and the counter: same style line ("charred dark wood counter, single warm side light from the left, near-black background, steam, no props, no text") with a different bowl each time.

## The Hollow House (Halloween)

**1. The gate (first frame)**
> Photoreal cinematic film still, Arri Alexa, anamorphic lens. Night. A tall decaying Victorian mansion on a low hill, seen straight on from outside a rusted wrought-iron gate that fills the foreground and stands slightly ajar. A narrow gravel path leads from the gate up to the porch and a heavy dark front door. Thick low ground fog rolling across the lawn, bare twisted trees on both sides, a full moon behind thin clouds giving cold blue rim light. One upstairs window glows a sickly amber, every other window is black. A few carved pumpkins glowing on the porch steps. Deep shadows, desaturated blue-green palette with small warm accents, film grain, no people, no text, no logo. The front door sits in the exact centre of the frame.

**2. The open door (last frame of act 1), gate still as reference**
> Same mansion, same night, same moonlight, fog and colour grade as the reference, but the camera has travelled all the way up the gravel path and onto the porch. Eye-level shot straight through the heavy front door, which now stands wide open in the centre of the frame. Through it, a long narrow Victorian hallway: peeling damask wallpaper, a threadbare red runner, a row of flickering candles in wall sconces, crooked portraits. At the far end of the hallway, small and blurred, a pale figure in a long white nightgown stands perfectly still, face hidden in shadow. Two carved pumpkins glow on the porch boards at the bottom corners. Cold blue outside, warm sick amber inside. Photoreal cinematic film still, anamorphic, film grain, no text.

**3. The approach (Kling, start = gate, end = open door)**
> One continuous slow steadicam dolly forward, no cuts. The camera glides through the open rusted iron gate, travels up the gravel path through rolling ground fog toward the mansion, climbs the porch steps past glowing carved pumpkins, and stops at the front door, which slowly creaks open by itself, revealing a long candlelit hallway with a pale figure standing still at the far end. Bare branches sway, fog drifts, candles flicker. Photoreal horror film, cold moonlight outside, warm amber inside, film grain, no people other than the distant figure, no text.

**4. The scare face (last frame of act 2), open-door still as reference**
> Same candlelit Victorian hallway, same colour grade and film grain as the reference, but the camera is now deep inside the hallway and the pale woman in the long white nightgown has rushed right up to the lens. Extreme close-up of her face filling the centre of the frame: grey-white skin, wet black hair hanging over half her face, completely black eyes, cracked lips slightly open. Candles burning out of focus behind her. Classic horror film jump scare frame, unsettling not gory, photoreal, anamorphic, no text.

**5. The hallway (Kling, start = open door, end = face)**
> One continuous shot, no cuts. The camera slowly steps over the threshold and creeps down the long candlelit hallway. The candles on the walls go out one by one as the camera passes. The pale woman in the white nightgown at the far end stays perfectly still, then suddenly sprints straight at the camera with unnatural speed and stops inches from the lens, her black eyes staring. Photoreal horror film, warm amber candlelight, film grain, handheld tension, no text.

**6. The hidden ghosts (one per room, room photo as reference)**
> Exactly the same photograph as the reference: identical camera angle, framing, lens, [room], lighting. The only change: [her in the rocking chair, head bowed / a ninth guest at the far end of the table / a pale hand gripping the stair rail / her reflected in the left mirror only]. Photoreal, film grain, no text.
