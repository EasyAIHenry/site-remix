# The Hollow House — creative brief

**What:** a made-up Halloween haunted-house attraction, 17 Oct to 2 Nov 2026, Singapore. Concept site for a reel. Footer says it's a concept.

**The bar:** Awwwards Site of the Day, horror-film title sequence. The standard: a cinematic AI film you drive with your scroll. Every stretch of scroll must move something or build dread. It has to make someone flinch.

**The one moment:** "you scroll up to the door, it opens, you creep down the hallway and she runs at you."

## Feel
A horror film's opening titles. Black, cold moonlight blue, sick amber candlelight, ONE accent: dried-blood red (~#7a0d0d to #a3120f) used sparingly. Film grain, a soft vignette, a faint flicker on the type. Display type: a sharp, slightly unsettling serif (e.g. Cormorant / IM Fell English / Pirata One for the wordmark only); small labels in a typewriter or mono. Tight, quiet copy. No cartoon Halloween, no emoji, no em dashes, no "spooky" clip art.

Signature interaction: a **flashlight cursor** in the darker sections (the page is near-black and a soft circle of light follows the pointer, revealing the room images and text). On touch, the light follows the scroll position instead.

## Scroll story (in order)
1. **The approach (pinned, ~300vh).** `clip-approach.mp4`: through the iron gate, up the foggy path, the door creaks open on a candlelit hallway with a figure at the end. Wordmark THE HOLLOW HOUSE over the sky at the start, fading as you move. Lines appear on cue, one at a time: "They closed it in 1926." · "The family never left." · "Seventeen nights only." The door opening is the peak of act 1.
2. **The hallway (pinned, ~250vh).** `clip-hallway.mp4`: the camera creeps in, candles go out one by one, she sprints at the lens. At the very end of the scrub: a one-frame red flash, a short screen shake, and `face_a.png` holds full-bleed for a beat. Then cut to black and one line: "Still want a ticket?"
3. **The rooms.** Four rooms, revealed by the flashlight: The Nursery (`room-nursery.png`), The Dining Room (`room-dining.png`), The Cellar (`room-cellar.png`), The Mirror Hall (`room-mirrors.png`). One sentence each, written like a case file.
4. **Tickets.** Nights 17 Oct to 2 Nov, 7pm to 1am, entry every 15 minutes, groups of up to 6. S$38 general, S$58 "Lights Out" (no torch, after 11pm). "Not for under 13."
5. **Close.** One button: "Book your night". Footer: "The Hollow House is a concept by Henry Chua. Not a real event."

## Assets (all AI, Higgsfield: GPT Image 2.5 + Kling 3.0)
`clip-approach.mp4` (10 s, gate to open door), `clip-hallway.mp4` (10 s, hallway to face), `start.png` (gate), `end.png` (open door), `face_a.png` (scare), `room-*.png`.

## THE STORY (Henry, 7 Oct: the site must tell one story; animation and words follow it)

**"The Hollows never left."** The page is a police case file from 1926 that you walk into. Copy is written as case notes (typewriter labels, file numbers, redaction bars, a CASE NO. 1926-1031 stamp). The scroll is you, the visitor, walking the same route the last officer took.

- **Act 1, the approach (clip-approach).** File opens: "31 October 1926. The Hollow family hosted a dinner for eight. The guests arrived. Nobody came out." Lines land as you move up the path: "The police found the gate open." · "The candles were still lit." · The door opens: "So was the door."
- **Act 2, the hallway (clip-hallway).** "Officer's last note: the daughter, Eliza, was seen in the hall." Candles go out as you scroll. She runs. Red flash, shake, her face. Black. "Eliza still walks the hall. Seventeen nights a year, so do you."
- **Evidence (the rooms, flashlight reveal).** Each room is an evidence card with an item number:
  - Exhibit A, The Nursery: "Eliza's chair rocks on its own. We stopped writing it down."
  - Exhibit B, The Dining Room: "Dinner for eight. Eight chairs. A ninth place setting nobody laid."
  - Exhibit C, The Cellar: "Where the search stopped. The officers would not say why."
  - Exhibit D, The Mirror Hall: "Count the guests in the reflection. Then count them in the room."
- **The house reopens (tickets).** "The house reopens for seventeen nights." Dates, times, prices, rules written as the conditions of entry.
- **Close.** "Case still open." Button: Book your night.

Every line, stamp and effect belongs to this one case. Nothing generic.
